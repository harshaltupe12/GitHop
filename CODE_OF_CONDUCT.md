# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GitHop to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. We pledge to make taking part in it a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use GitHop
- Owning mistakes and learning from them

Things that aren't acceptable:

- Insults, personal attacks, or sexualised language and imagery
- Trolling, harassment, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by contacting [@harshaltupe12](https://github.com/harshaltupe12). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
